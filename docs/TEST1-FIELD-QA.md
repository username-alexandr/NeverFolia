## Frozen automatic thresholds

These thresholds are intentionally looser than the accepted `2f680ee` baseline so normal world variation has headroom while the original pathological shapes still fail.

- maximum vertical open run: `<=160` blocks
- maximum identical partial layer-mask run: `<=32` blocks
- flooded tree blocks in sampled flood columns: `0`
- flooded rail blocks in sampled flood columns: `0`
- flooded sugar cane / lily pad blocks in sampled flood columns: `0`
- resource ore blocks in `Y=65..135`: `0`

Accepted `2f680ee` topology baseline:

- FULL chunks scanned: `326`
- max vertical open run: `124`
- max identical partial mask run: `18`
- chunks containing columns `>=192`: `0`
- chunks containing columns `>=256`: `0`
- mean open fraction: `0.035807`

## Manual repeat test

Use a completely fresh world. Do not reuse chunks generated by the first TEST1 package.

1. Install the frozen `2f680ee` Paperclip JAR and both final datapack ZIPs before first world generation.
2. Start with no NeverLand production plugins. Confirm `/version`. Folia intentionally disables the vanilla `/datapack` command, so datapack activation must be verified from startup/fingerprint logs rather than `/datapack list`.
3. Run `/profiler world <x> <y> <z> 10 100` in a loaded area and confirm it starts instead of printing usage/error.
4. Fly across fresh Overworld terrain at the Y=128 flood boundary. Look specifically for vertical terrain walls, repeating rectangular cave cuts, drowned tree remnants, underwater rails and vegetation.
5. Inspect fresh stone around `Y=65..135`; normal resource ore veins should not appear in this transition band.
6. Check deep terrain around `Y=-100`, `-200`, `-300`, `-400`, and near the bottom. Confirm caves remain varied and do not form continuous surface-to-bottom shafts.
7. Locate/generate Trial Chambers and verify the structure is inside approximately `Y=-320..-96`.
8. Locate/generate mineshafts and verify they remain in the deep profile rather than returning to the flooded surface domain.
9. Confirm vanilla stronghold/End Portal progression is absent.
10. Locate several villages and Woodland Mansions around flooded regions. They must not start submerged or extend materially into the flooded lowland.
11. Locate a swamp hut. It should remain a valid swamp feature but sit at the new flooded waterline rather than at the old drowned ground level.
12. Enter NeverNether through a normal portal, inspect fresh chunks, and return through the portal.

When reporting a failure, capture: exact coordinates, F3 Y-level, structure ID if applicable, screenshot, and the relevant server-log interval. For worldgen visual defects, preserve the seed and do not regenerate the affected chunk until diagnosis is complete.

## NeverNether manual field observations — 2026-09-07

Manual TEST1 inspection of fresh NeverNether chunks reported no visible generation bugs.

- observed primary lava surface: approximately `Y=30`; target is `Y=32`, so this is within the intended local terrain/surface variation around the configured primary lava level;
- the roof is directly walkable at player feet `Y≈381.4` in the inspected location; this is the practical top surface of the irregular bedrock roof, not merely the Y-coordinate of a buried bedrock block;
- the generated terrain body still intentionally closes near the `Y=383` contract boundary, with local irregular bedrock thickness accounting for the walkable surface around `Y=381`;
- manual building was confirmed at least through `Y=667` before the tester stopped the tower test;
- the client/server build-limit message confirms a maximum construction height of `Y=895`, matching the dimension contract `min_y=-128`, `height=1024`;
- intended roof construction space therefore extends from immediately above the bedrock roof through the legal top of the dimension, with the exact lower usable Y depending on local bedrock irregularity.

Current field verdict for NeverNether terrain and vertical limits: **PASS**.

## NeverOverworld underground manual observations — 2026-09-07

Manual inspection of the current TEST1 Overworld underground shows a substantially vanilla-like cave topology again.

- large caverns are irregular and organically connected instead of appearing as long rectangular or extruded masks;
- dripstone caves render naturally with varied floors, ceilings, pillars and local openings;
- deep deepslate/sculk terrain and ore exposure coexist normally with the cave network;
- very large open spaces still occur, but in the inspected areas they read as natural Minecraft cave systems rather than the pathological surface-to-bottom shafts seen in the earlier regression;
- no obvious repeated chunk-edge cave pattern or giant straight vertical canyon is visible in the supplied field screenshots.

Current manual field verdict for NeverOverworld cave topology: **PASS**.

Note: this verdict is specifically for cave/underground terrain topology. The explicit abandoned-mineshaft placement/depth policy remains a separate structure check unless an actual `minecraft:mineshaft` start is inspected.
